# Divino Bitcoin Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Divino Bitcoin to be a community where people can contribute, question assumptions, report risks, and learn without being subjected to harassment or manipulation. We welcome good-faith participation regardless of experience, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language, focus criticism on ideas and evidence, disclose conflicts of interest, protect private information, and accept that maintainers may enforce documented safety boundaries. Credit the work of others and ask before moving a private conversation or personal account information into a public space.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, impersonation, fraud, spam, unsolicited mass outreach, token promotion, investment solicitation, vote manipulation, deliberate misinformation, publication of vulnerabilities before responsible coordination, and attempts to pressure contributors or maintainers into bypassing security gates are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, official chat spaces, events, and private communication related to the project. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban participants when necessary to protect the community and the project.

Report a concern privately to `contatodivinobitcoin@proton.me`. Do not send seeds, private keys, recovery material, passwords, authentication codes, or other secrets. Security vulnerabilities must be reported according to `SECURITY.md`.

Enforcement decisions will be guided by the seriousness of the conduct, its impact, available evidence, and the need to protect contributors and users. The project will aim to be fair, proportionate, and transparent without exposing reporters or sensitive details.
