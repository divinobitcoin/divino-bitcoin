# Divino Bitcoin Foundations

> **Status:** public statement. It describes project principles, not a promise of enabled capabilities.

Divino Bitcoin is an open-source, self-custodial Bitcoin wallet project. It is motivated by the belief that people deserve meaningful financial sovereignty, software they can inspect, and tools that do not require blind trust in an intermediary. The project is licensed under **GPL-3.0-or-later** because freedom to study, use, modify, and share the software is part of its purpose.

## What we stand for

| Principle | Public commitment |
|---|---|
| **User sovereignty** | A wallet should strengthen a user’s control rather than obscure custody or move trust to an unaccountable third party. |
| **Free and auditable software** | Source code, architectural decisions, security limits, and public claims should be open to inspection and independent criticism. |
| **Decentralization** | We prefer designs that reduce unnecessary dependencies, concentrated control, and single points of failure. |
| **Security with evidence** | Security is not a slogan. Sensitive capabilities require scoped design, documented gates, tests, review, and observable evidence. |
| **Honest scope** | We will describe what the software does today, what remains blocked, and what still requires review. We will not imply Mainnet readiness, custody safety, or Lightning capability before they are actually established. |
| **Serious community participation** | Good-faith suggestions, tests, documentation, translations, design work, security review, and code contributions are welcome. Criticism is valuable when it is specific, respectful, and evidence-based. |

## How we work with the community

The project aims to learn from the culture that has made free and open-source software durable: people can inspect the work, identify flaws, propose improvements, and help maintain standards. Participation is open, but openness is not permission for disorder or harm.

We do not accept spam, fraud, harassment, discrimination, token promotion, investment solicitation, manipulation of community platforms, or pressure to bypass security gates. A contribution should improve the project, clarify a risk, test a claim, or help users understand their choices. Vulnerability reports must follow the responsible-disclosure policy in `SECURITY.md`.

## The standard we accept for ourselves

The project’s public commitments must guide its decisions. When convenience conflicts with custody, when growth conflicts with safety, or when speed conflicts with reviewability, we will explain the trade-off and preserve the security boundary. Community input may change our implementation; it must never be used to erase necessary safeguards.

This document is deliberately a statement of principles rather than a promise of capabilities. The current application remains in local demonstration mode, with Signet as its only development environment. Mainnet, real funds, seeds, private keys, recovery, signing, broadcast, operational endpoints, and active Lightning remain blocked until formal gates, independent review, and evidence justify any change.

## Living commitment

This declaration can evolve through public discussion and serious contribution. Changes should preserve its central promise: **the Divino Bitcoin project must be as accountable in its practice as it is ambitious in its ideals.**
